About a year ago, this was my reality.

An idea would come to me at two in the morning, and I'd immediately get out of bed, excited, to write it down.

The next day, I'd start the morning full of excitement, decide on the tech stack, and immediately begin preparing the project documentation.

After writing down almost every feature and creating a complete roadmap, I'd start building the project's foundation.

But somehow, because of how busy my work and life were, I could never manage to finish these projects and launch them.

I was so unlucky. Life was unfair to me. If only I had a year to myself, I had all the knowledge and energy I needed to build an app that the whole world would talk about, one that would make me and my entire family rich.

I never managed to do it...

Thankfully, we now have artificial intelligence. It brainstorms with me, and I can build applications without writing a single line of code, just by doing quick checks along the way.

I think I had around 20 projects that I'd started but never finished up until a year ago.

Now things are very different.

Over the past year, I've started and failed to finish more than 100 projects. Ideas are flying around. Tokens are flowing like water, but there are no results.

I'm sure I'm not alone in this. There must be plenty of people in the same situation.

I think the easiest thing to do is to label this as perfectionism, ADHD, or something similar.

This recurring psychological pattern, in my opinion, needs to be changed and fixed as soon as possible.
