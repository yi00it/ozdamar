After two years, I wrote my first article for teknoloji.org today. And I have to say, it feels pretty good.

I want to be much more proactive for the rest of the year. I want to develop apps, work on marketing them, and at the same time, write about them on teknoloji.org.

I also need to go back and update some of the older articles.
