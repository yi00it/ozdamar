Sahife is a calm, private, fast task manager for everyday plans. The name comes from an old word for a page, a written sheet.

## Principles

- **Local-first.** Every change is saved on your device first and synced in the background.
- **End-to-end encrypted.** The server stores only ciphertext and never sees your tasks.
- **Quiet.** No noise, no gamification. Just your tasks, in order.

Sahife is free on the web. The private alpha is open by invitation.
